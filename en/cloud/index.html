What Is a Public Cloud Architecture Solution?

A public cloud architecture solution means designing a system architecture that uses the compute, storage, and networking resources of providers like AWS, GCP, and Azure to match your business needs. Compared to building your own facility, public cloud offers rapid deployment, elastic scaling, and pay-as-you-go pricing — but without careful architecture planning, it can just as easily lead to idle resources, runaway costs, or security misconfigurations.

Kineticlinks' cloud consultants bring hands-on multi-cloud integration experience, helping you evaluate whether a single-cloud, multi-cloud, or hybrid cloud approach fits your situation, and designing for high availability, disaster recovery, and data sovereignty requirements — while also setting up foundational security controls like security groups, identity permissions, and network segmentation.

For migration projects, we plan a phased migration strategy based on system dependencies to minimize downtime and business risk. After migration, we continue to provide usage analysis and cost optimization recommendations, helping you avoid unnecessary cloud spend and keep budget focused on the resources that actually drive business growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

I have zero cloud experience — can you plan this from scratch?

Yes. We start by understanding your current architecture and business needs, then provide full consulting and hands-on support from initial cloud service selection through architecture design and ongoing operations.

Will a cloud migration cause service downtime?

We plan a phased migration strategy based on system dependencies and test and validate everything before the official cutover, minimizing downtime — critical systems can also run in parallel during the transition to ensure a smooth handoff.

When does hybrid cloud make more sense than going fully public cloud?

If your organization has regulatory requirements to keep data on-premises, hasn't finished depreciating existing hardware investments, or has systems that are extremely latency-sensitive, a hybrid architecture lets public cloud and your own facility complement each other.

Can you help optimize an existing cloud bill?

Yes. We audit current resource usage to identify idle resources, oversized instances, or opportunities like reserved instances that reduce cost, and provide concrete optimization recommendations.

Is cloud architecture consulting a one-time project or an ongoing engagement?

Both models are available. You can opt for a single architecture-planning-and-migration project, or an ongoing consulting arrangement where we continue monitoring architecture health and cost efficiency.
