Frequently Asked Questions About Kineticlinks

If your question isn't answered here, get in touch directly

What services does Kineticlinks provide?

Four core services: CDN content delivery, VPS hosting, IDC data center buildout, and public cloud architecture solutions. We also operate two platforms of our own: an SSL certificate automation platform and a global 3-network speed test platform.

Which regions do you cover?

Primarily Taiwan and Asia-Pacific, with CDN nodes also across North America and Europe. Node density is deliberately strongest around Taiwan, Hong Kong, and mainland China, which suits businesses with cross-border visitors.

I'm not sure whether I need a VPS or my own facility. Can I consult first?

Yes. Most clients are unsure at the outset. Share your current traffic volume, application type, and budget, and our architects will help you assess the options — no commitment required.

How long does onboarding take?

It varies considerably by service. CDN is usually a DNS change completed within a business day; VPS can be provisioned immediately; IDC buildout runs from several weeks to a few months depending on scale, with a concrete schedule provided during assessment.

Do you offer Chinese-language technical support?

Yes. In an emergency you reach a person rather than only an automated ticket system, and Chinese-language contact has no timezone gap. Facilities and network are monitored around the clock by the upstream NOC; we handle the Chinese-language side and coordination. We are small, so the person you reach is the person who handles it.
