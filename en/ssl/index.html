What Is an SSL Certificate Automation Platform?

An SSL certificate is required to enable HTTPS on a website, but the traditional request, validation, and renewal process is often manual: creating DNS validation records by hand, downloading certificate files, and uploading them one by one to servers or CDNs. Certificates typically need reissuing every few months, and a single oversight can leave a site showing a security warning after expiration.

The Kineticlinks SSL automation platform ties the whole process into a single interface. It integrates with the APIs of major DNS providers like GoDaddy to complete domain ownership validation automatically, removing the need to manually add TXT or CNAME records. Once a certificate is issued, it can also be pushed automatically to multiple CDN brands, connecting certificate deployment directly to your CDN acceleration setup.

The platform also provides expiration reminders and automatic renewal, reducing the risk of a certificate lapsing due to human oversight — especially useful for teams managing many domains or frequently switching certificates between CDN providers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What domain permissions do I need to use this platform?

You need DNS management access to the domain — the platform creates validation records automatically through its integrated DNS provider APIs. If your DNS provider isn't yet supported, manual validation is also available.

Will the platform notify me before a certificate expires?

Yes. The platform sends proactive reminders before expiration, and you can enable automatic renewal to reduce the risk of a lapsed certificate causing a security warning.

Can issued certificates be used on hosting or CDNs outside of Kineticlinks?

Yes. The platform issues standard SSL certificates that can be downloaded and used on any server or CDN that supports SSL — they aren't locked to Kineticlinks services.

Which DNS providers support automatic validation?

We currently integrate with major DNS providers including GoDaddy, and are continuing to expand support. If your provider isn't yet supported, reach out and we'll evaluate adding it.

Is the platform free? How is it charged?

Platform features carry no separate charge — the cost is the certificate itself: US$30/year for a single-domain certificate and US$90/year for a wildcard, with automatic reissue within the term at no extra cost. There is no monthly or account fee, and your first certificate is fully refundable if it doesn't fit.
