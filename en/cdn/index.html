What Is a CDN?

A CDN (Content Delivery Network) is a network of edge servers distributed across multiple regions. When a user visits your site, the CDN routes the request to the node physically closest to them instead of forcing every request back to a single origin server, shortening the data path, reducing latency, and easing the load on your origin infrastructure.

Kineticlinks operates over 100 edge nodes across Asia-Pacific, North America, and Europe, using intelligent routing to continuously measure latency and congestion at each node and automatically select the fastest path. Whether your users are in Taiwan, mainland China, Southeast Asia, or Western markets, they get access speeds close to a local server. How much that actually delivers depends heavily on the caching headers your origin sends — set wrongly, they leave a CDN doing almost nothing, as our guide to HTTP caching headers explains.

A public service is also the first line against denial-of-service attacks. The upstream network we resell can scrub at terabit scale, filtering attack traffic at the edge before it reaches your origin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a CDN worth it for a small website?

Yes. CDN benefits aren't limited to large sites — even a small or mid-sized site with visitors overseas or in other regions will see a noticeable improvement in load time and user experience.

Is CDN setup complicated? Do I need to change hosts or code?

No hosting change is required. In most cases you only need to update your domain's DNS to route traffic to the CDN — your origin server and codebase stay untouched, and setup is usually completed within a business day.

Does the CDN also protect against DDoS attacks?

Yes. The upstream CDN we resell scrubs at terabit scale, filtering malicious traffic at the edge before it reaches your origin. What any given plan absorbs depends on the plan, so we confirm your traffic profile first.

Which regions does the CDN cover?

We currently operate nodes across Asia-Pacific (including Taiwan, Hong Kong, and areas around mainland China), North America, and Europe, with coverage expanding based on customer needs and a particular focus on access quality in the Taiwan and China markets.

How do I know which CDN plan I need?

Share your current traffic volume, where your visitors are located, and your content type with us, and our architects will recommend a node configuration and pricing plan that fits.
